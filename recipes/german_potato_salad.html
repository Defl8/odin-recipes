Authentic German Potato Salad

Description

German potato salad tossed in flavourful and tangy bacon vinaigrette.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups diced peeled potatoes
  • 4 slices bacon
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons white sugar, or more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Gather the ingredients

  2. Step 2

    Place potatoes in a large pot and add water to cover by at least 1 to 2 inches; bring to a boil. Cook until potatoes are easily pierced with a fork, about 10 minutes. Drain, and set aside to cool.

  3. Step 3

    While the potatoes are cooking, place bacon in a large, deep skillet over medium-high heat. Fry until browned and crisp, 10 to 12 minutes, turning as needed. Transfer bacon to a poper towel-lined plate and crumble when cool enough to handle. Leave bacon grease in the skillet.

  4. Step 4

    Cook onion in the bacon grease over medium heat until browned, 6 to 8 minutes. Add vinegar, sugar, water, salt, and pepper to the pan and bring to a boil.

  5. Step 5

    Once boiling, add potatoes, and 1/2 of the crumbled bacon. Cook until heated through, 3 to 4 minutes.

  6. Step 6

    Transfer warm potato salad to a serving dish and sprinkle remaining crumbled bacon over the top. Garnish with parsley and serve warm.
